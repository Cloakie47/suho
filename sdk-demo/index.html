Suhoswap a demo dApp on GIWA

Sign in with Suho

This page uses @suho/ondol. No wallet extension, no seed phrase. One passkey tap in a Suho popup, and first-time users onboard right inside it.

1 · Connect + sign in

Opens the Suho popup, connects your account, and proves it with a SIWE signature verified on-chain via ERC-1271.

2 · Sign a message

Sign any text with your passkey. The demo verifies the signature on-chain and shows the result.
